## Why a Blog?

I've always believed that the best way to solidify knowledge is to write about it. Whether I've just built something interesting, stumbled upon an unexpected bug, or found a clever trick I haven't seen documented anywhere — this is the place where that ends up.

This isn't meant to be a polished publication. It's a living notebook: raw, direct, and hopefully useful to anyone who finds their way here.

## What to Expect

- **Projects** — Deep dives into things I've built, the decisions behind them, and what I'd do differently.
- **Discoveries** — Interesting findings from reverse engineering sessions, code audits, or just tinkering.
- **Tutorials** — Practical step-by-step guides on tools, techniques, or concepts I've found valuable.
- **Opinions** — My unfiltered take on things in the software and security world.
